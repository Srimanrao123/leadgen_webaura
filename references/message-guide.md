# Webaura Outreach Message Guide

This guide defines how Webaura (`https://webauraindia.com`) crafts outreach messages across WhatsApp and Email.

---

## The Anti-AI & Human Realism Rules (Must Follow)

Outreach messages must feel like they were written by a real person on their phone—not generated by an AI assistant or copied from a generic corporate template.

### 1. Kill the Corporate / AI Tone
- ❌ **No em dashes (`—`)**: Em dashes scream "AI generated". Use commas, hyphens (`-`), or separate lines.
- ❌ **No AI apology / excuse loops**: NEVER say:
  - *"Sorry for the inconvenience earlier — you do already have a website at..."*
  - *"It was not opening on our end due to some technical issue..."*
  Speak naturally, like a human replying on WhatsApp.
- ❌ **No "Service Buffet" / Feature Vomit**: NEVER list 5 services in one cold message:
  - *"Beyond the website, we also build automations, CRMs and dashboards that make day-to-day operations easier... We also handle digital marketing — Google Ads and Meta Ads..."*
  Listing everything at once looks like spam.
  **Rule: 1 message = 1 specific observation + 1 clear solution.**
- ❌ **No robotic openers or fake flattery**:
  - ❌ *"I hope this message finds you well"*
  - ❌ *"I am reaching out to introduce Webaura, a premier digital agency..."*
  - ❌ *"Your prestigious institution is renowned for excellence..."*
- ❌ **No lazy, passive CTAs**:
  - ❌ *"Would you like a short overview of any of these?"*
  - ❌ *"Let me know your thoughts."*
  - ❌ *"Looking forward to hearing from you."*

### 2. Make Every URL Clickable (Strict Rule)
- On WhatsApp and mobile email, bare domains like `webauraindia.com` or `treatmentrangehospitals.com` **do not turn into clickable links**; they render as flat, unclickable text.
- **Always include the full protocol:** `https://webauraindia.com` (NEVER `webauraindia.com`).
- Put the agency URL on its own line in the signature so WhatsApp renders a clean, 1-tap clickable preview.
- Any link to a client's website, sample demo, or video must also have `https://`.

### 3. Keep WhatsApp Messages Short & Thumb-Friendly
- **Length:** 40 to 90 words max (3 to 5 short lines).
- Indian business owners, doctors, and builders read WhatsApp while moving between meetings. If it looks like a wall of text, it gets skipped.
- Use natural line breaks between thoughts.

### 4. Standard Sign-off Format
Always use the sender's real name (from CRM `whoami`), Webaura, and the full clickable URL:
```text
Regards,
Gopal | Webaura
https://webauraindia.com
```

## Email Versions

For email, use the same core message with:
1. **A short, curiosity-inducing subject line** (3–6 words, no spam triggers):
   - `Quick question regarding [Company Name]`
   - `WhatsApp inquiry flow for [Clinic Name]`
   - `[Project Name] - buyer inquiry tracking`
2. Keep the body concise (under 120 words).
3. Use clean formatting with clickable links: `https://webauraindia.com`.
