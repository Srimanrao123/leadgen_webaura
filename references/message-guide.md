# Writing the message

## Before writing, answer these privately
What does this clinic specialize in? Who is their likely patient? Which treatments matter most?
What does their content look like now? What is missing? Why would *this* clinic care?
Only use observations you have evidence for.

## Message types (pick the one that fits, and vary them)
| Type | Use when | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| A. Specific problem | A clear, verifiable gap (tiny Instagram, empty video section) | Observation → problem → how we help → CTA |
| B. Missed opportunity | Strong services, but content isn't used well | Observation → opportunity → solution → CTA |
| C. Personalized content ideas | You can name topics from their own blog/FAQ | Research → 2–3 concrete video ideas → offer → CTA |
| D. Doctor personal brand | The doctor has strong expertise or teaches | Expertise → personal-brand angle → solution → CTA |
| E. Production burden | They're active, but keeping it up is hard | Observation → effort it takes → end-to-end help → CTA |
| F. Direct offer | Only if nothing specific was found | Short offer → CTA (avoid where possible) |

## Rules
- **Short:** 90–150 words. Observation, opportunity, how we help, one question.
- **2–3 specific details** in every message: the doctor's name, a real blog title, a follower count, a named treatment, the road/area.
- **AI is the mechanism, not the pitch.** Say "we handle scripting, video production in your voice, editing and Reels ready to post. Your team only approves." Not "revolutionary AI technology".
- **Never:** "I hope this email finds you well", "I am reaching out to introduce", "leading company", fake compliments, piles of emojis, long service lists.
- **CTA (default):** "Should I share a sample Reel so you can see how it looks?" Alternatives: "Would you like 3 video topic ideas for your clinic?" or "Should I send 3 sample scripts?"
- **Sign-off:** the sender's name, with the business name on the next line.
- A polite **P.S.** about a real website issue (template text, broken social links) builds trust. Use it at most once and only when it's verified.

## Example (fictional clinic, for style only)
Subject: Your aligner FAQ as short videos

> Hi Dr. Rao,
>
> Your FAQ on smileplus.in already answers what patients ask most about aligners: how long, how many hours a day, what it costs. But the "Videos" section on the site is empty, and @smileplusdental has about 400 followers.
>
> Those same questions work really well as 30-second Reels with you explaining them. We handle it end to end: script from your FAQ, video in your voice, editing, Reels ready to post. Your team only approves.
>
> Should I share a sample Reel so you can see how it looks?
>
> Asha
> BrightFrame Studio

## WhatsApp version
Use the same text with no subject line. Keep the line breaks. WhatsApp formatting: `*bold*`, `_italic_`. Don't paste tables; turn them into short lines.

## Sample scripts (when a clinic asks)
35–45 seconds, written for the doctor to speak. Open with a concrete, surprising line, not "Hi everyone". Put one idea per line, give each beat a visual, and end with one CTA. Stay NMC-safe: educational, with no promised results, no before/afters and no price claims. Ask for the doctor's review before production.
